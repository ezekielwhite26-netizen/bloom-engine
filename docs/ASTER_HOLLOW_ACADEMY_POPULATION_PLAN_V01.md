## Florence school-world shape

Florence should have a school network broader than the focal magical cohort. The initial recurring population should be socially complete enough to support lunch, class transitions, Student Council/event work, ordinary conflict, study, arts/music, athletics, and after-school life without naming hundreds of students.

Target recurring junior set: approximately 30-40 total people, with overlap across categories.

## Durable world-fill examples

Once the owning-core data exists, ordinary structural facts may become durable generated canon:

- a student's recurring third-period class;
- a teacher assigned to a course section;
- membership in Student Council or an ordinary club;
- ordinary acquaintance/familiarity created by sustained shared activity.

Once persisted, those facts are retrieved and reused rather than rerolled.

Protected facts remain user-controlled, including romance, major intimacy, betrayal, secret magical knowledge, major family connection, major personality change, or plot-relevant hidden motive.
